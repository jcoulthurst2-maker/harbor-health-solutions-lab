# AI Approach

AI is not a separate course inside Harbor Health, and it is not automatically the answer.

## AI helps with the build

I make the first attempt, run it, inspect what happened, and use AI for the smallest useful help when needed. I still have to fix the work, understand it, and explain why it works.

## AI may become part of the hospital

Before adding AI to a workflow, I first ask whether normal software, SQL, business rules, or automation can solve the problem more safely and reliably.

If AI is justified, the design must answer:

- What data can the AI see?
- What decision is it allowed to make?
- What still requires a human?
- What happens when the AI is wrong?
- What happens when the AI is unavailable?
- What is logged?
- How are overrides handled?
- How is performance monitored after launch?

**Sometimes the right answer is not to use AI at all.**
